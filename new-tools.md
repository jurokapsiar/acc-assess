# Proposed tooling

These are gaps in automated coverage for WCAG 2.2 A/AA in the `accessibility-assessment` skill. Today these criteria rely on agent review or a human (see `skills/accessibility-assessment/references/criteria-coverage.md`). Most proposals are self-contained Playwright-based checks that would plug into `assess.mjs` as a new `--checks` entry. #2 is now implemented as a separate skill (see below).

| # | Tool | Criteria | Approach | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **APG keyboard conformance runner** | 2.1.1, 4.1.2, APG suggestions | Turn each scraped `knowledge/apg/<slug>.md` *Keyboard Interaction* and *Roles, States, Properties* section into a machine-readable spec (an LLM-assisted, one-off conversion stored in `knowledge/`). Then drive every widget on a screen that matches the pattern's role and assert the focus/state change after each key. | L |
| 2 | **Screen reader automation** — *implemented:* [`skills/screen-reader-validation`](skills/screen-reader-validation/SKILL.md) | 1.1.1, 1.3.1, 1.3.2, 2.4.1, 2.4.3, 3.3.1, 4.1.2, 4.1.3 | [Guidepup](https://www.guidepup.dev/) with NVDA (Windows) or VoiceOver (macOS) replays scope-definition journeys, records the narration per step and flags unnamed/roleless controls, unannounced states, dialogs, status messages and errors, focus not moved/returned, silent navigation, reading order and missing landmarks/headings (`sr-*` rules in [rules.md](rules.md)). Its findings use the assessment finding shape but are not yet merged into `report.html` automatically. Remaining: merge into the main report; expected-announcement assertions per journey step (2.4.6). | L |
| 3 | **Non-text contrast analyser** | 1.4.11 | For every focusable/interactive element, sample border, icon and focus-ring pixels from focused and unfocused screenshots against adjacent background pixels; report ratios below 3:1. | M |
| 4 | **Hover/focus content tester** | 1.4.13 | Hover and focus every element with `aria-describedby`, `title`, `[popover]` or a tooltip role. Assert that Escape dismisses the content without moving the pointer, the pointer can move onto it, and it persists for 1 s without interaction. | S |
| 5 | **Text resize checker** | 1.4.4 | Emulate 200 % zoom (viewport at half width) and, separately, set root `font-size: 200%`. Detect clipped text (`scrollWidth > clientWidth` with `overflow: hidden`), overlapping bounding boxes and truncated controls; screenshot the diff. | S |
| 6 | **Orientation check** | 1.3.4 | Re-run each mobile target in landscape and portrait. Flag content hidden or rotated, or "rotate your device" messages. | S |
| 7 | **Pointer gesture and cancellation probe** | 2.5.1, 2.5.2, 2.5.7 | Instrument `pointerdown`/`touchstart`/`mousedown` listeners (CDP `DOMDebugger.getEventListeners`). Flag actions that fire on down-events, and drag-only widgets (`draggable`, pointer-move handlers) without a click alternative. | M |
| 8 | **Context-change detector** | 3.2.1, 3.2.2 | During the keyboard walk and form filling, watch for navigation, new windows, focus jumps and form submission caused by focus or value change alone. | S |
| 9 | **Form error-flow fuzzer** | 3.3.1, 3.3.3, 3.3.4, 4.1.3 | Submit empty and invalid values per input type. Assert errors are shown as text, `aria-invalid` is set, the error is linked with `aria-describedby`, focus moves to or announces the first error, and a live region fires. | M |
| 10 | **Timing and motion monitor** | 2.2.1, 2.2.2, 2.3.1 | Record a 10 s video per screen. Detect auto-advancing or auto-updating regions (DOM mutation rate), missing pause controls, toasts that auto-dismiss in under 20 s, and flashes using a frame-difference luminance analysis (PEAT-like). | M |
| 11 | **Character key shortcut scanner** | 2.1.4 | Press each printable key with focus on `body` and record side effects (DOM, URL and focus changes). | S |
| 12 | **Cross-screen consistency checker** | 2.4.2, 2.4.5, 3.2.3, 3.2.4, 3.2.6 | Use the inventory: extract landmarks, nav order, `document.title`, help links and repeated control names per screen, then diff across screens. | S |
| 13 | **Redundant entry / authentication checker** | 3.3.7, 3.3.8 | Flows defined as inventory states: assert previously entered values are pre-filled or selectable, and that password/OTP fields allow paste and `autocomplete`. | S |
| 14 | **Forced colors / high contrast diff** | 1.4.11, 2.4.7 (Windows High Contrast) | Emulate `forcedColors: 'active'`; flag icons, focus indicators and borders that disappear (pixel diff against the normal render). | S |
| 15 | **Media alternatives inspector** | 1.2.1–1.2.5 | Enumerate `<video>`/`<audio>` elements and embedded players; check `<track kind=captions/descriptions>` and transcript links; extract captions for agent review of accuracy. | S |
| 16 | **Reading/focus order visual diff** | 1.3.2, 2.4.3 | Compare DOM/tab order with the visual reading order (bounding boxes sorted by row and column, RTL-aware); flag inversions above a threshold. Automates what the agent now judges from the tab-order screenshot. *Partly in place:* `assess.mjs` already records every tab stop's box (`results.json` `tabOrder[].box`) and the report's Scope section overlays them; only the comparison is missing. | M |

Suggested order (value ÷ effort): 4, 5, 8, 12, 3, 9, 1.
